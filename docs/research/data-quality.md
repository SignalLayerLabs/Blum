# Historical data acceptance

BLUM accepts a source for a particular use, never as a blanket certificate of
financial truth. The 2.2 release audited the Space's packaged prices, both public
Italianhype datasets, the relevant outcome snapshot branch and all 95 archived
reasoning snapshots in the persistent bucket. Source revisions and rejection
ledgers are retained in [the evidence directory](../evidence).

## Accepted and rejected inputs

| Source | Decision | Permitted use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Packaged equity/ETF prices | 75,381 of 75,386 rows accepted; five incoherent OHLC bars excluded | Price-based historical replay |
| Missing volume | Eight accepted price rows retain a missing volume value | Price analysis with an explicit completeness warning |
| Forex CSV | 71 unambiguous weekday snapshots accepted; 632 conflicting rows and 223 additional weekend rows quarantined | Limited historical inspection; one causal research example |
| Raw public reasoning dataset | 521 records quarantined for new training/promotion | Audit of previously published examples |
| Public outcome snapshot branch | Quarantined | Source/history inspection |
| Persistent reasoning snapshots | All 95 versions quarantined for new training/promotion | Audit and reproducibility investigation |
| Community Memory dataset | Zero contribution records | No financial evidence available |

The equity cache covers 30 assets from 2016-06-13 through 2026-06-12. Each
accepted row preserves its provider. Five records on 2023-06-05 have open prices
above their highs: IWM, XLF, XLE, JPM and XOM. BLUM excludes these observations
at bootstrap and when constructing replay frames from previously persisted
prices. It does not repair prices by clipping or inventing a candle.

The Forex CSV contains repeated pair/date keys with contradictory OHLC values.
BLUM rejects the entire conflicting group. It never merges those daily records
into a fabricated first-open/max-high/min-low/last-close candle. Identical OHLC
records are deduplicated. Weekend observations lack session/timezone provenance
and remain quarantined. Recomputed indicators and labels require a continuous
causal warmup/label path: no gap over four calendar days. One resulting example
cannot meet the validation minimums and cannot establish an edge.

Old external Forex feature rows and historical advice generated by transform
`blum-forex-daily-causal-v1` remain on disk for audit but are excluded from new
training scans. Transform `blum-forex-daily-quality-v2` has distinct source
identities and invalidates affected dataset hashes.

## Reasoning data is not market evidence

The main reasoning dataset passes its schema and content-hash checks. All 521
raw targets nevertheless contain numerical claims unavailable in their inputs;
all outcomes are inconclusive, 490 confidence values are 100, and only AAPL and
AMAT appear. The previously published sanitized derivative is reproducible:
all three file hashes and 3,095 replacements match its training manifest. This
does not certify independent market performance or authorize new training.

The public outcome snapshot has 29 outcome timestamps beyond its declared
cutoff and 170 training/validation outcomes crossing the next split boundary.
Its provenance objects are empty. Its validation and test reflections have a
single outcome class.

The 95 persistent archives pass all 285 declared file hashes and their snapshot
hashes, but all have empty provenance and outcomes after declared cutoffs. 92
also fail split-boundary availability checks, three have empty splits, and 94
have single-class holdouts. Their 21,424 record occurrences represent 530 unique
lineages, not 21,424 independent examples. 108 lineages move between split labels
across versions. Repeated snapshots are never counted as new forward evidence.

HF automatic training and dataset snapshot publication remain disabled for this
release. Restoring these requires verified provenance, availability timestamps,
purged splits and a fresh independent evaluation. Previously published model
files remain available as advisory research artifacts; this release makes no
new performance or promotion claim about them.

## Corporate actions and storage boundaries

Sampled split dates are consistent with back-adjusted prices. The cache has no
explicit adjustment factors, dividends or certified volume adjustment. Accepted
prices support declared price replay; they do not support dividend-inclusive
returns or historical nominal share accounting. Historical replay never becomes
paper-forward evidence simply because it is loaded into a current runtime.

The persistent bucket also contains approximately 5.6 GB of logical database
backup, 7.9 GB of SQL data and physical recovery files. They remain attached and
preserved. The archive audit does not certify their full contents. Existing
database rows retain their original timestamps and provenance and must pass the
appropriate feature/evaluation gates before entering a new experiment.

## Reproduce the packaged data audit

```bash
PYTHONPATH=backend python scripts/audit_historical_sources.py --check
```

This checks the exact reviewed file hashes and acceptance counts against
`backend/app/data/history_acceptance_policy.json`. A changed source or changed
acceptance rule requires an explicit review and a new policy revision. Data
integrity is necessary, but insufficient, for model validity.

Forex source attribution and its separate CC BY-SA 4.0 license are recorded in
[the source README](../../backend/app/data/forex/README.md). Provider terms and model
or dataset licenses must be evaluated separately from the application license.
