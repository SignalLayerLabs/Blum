# BLUM Support

Use the narrowest public channel that fits the request.

## Reproducible defects

Open a [bug report](https://github.com/SignalLayerLabs/Blum/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml)
with the affected revision, reproduction steps, observed behavior and
non-sensitive logs. Do not include API keys, database credentials, broker data
or personal financial information.

## Research and usage questions

Use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/SignalLayerLabs/Blum/discussions)
for installation help, architecture questions, research methodology and ideas
that do not yet have measured evidence.

## Evidence-driven proposals

Use the [proposal form](https://github.com/SignalLayerLabs/Blum/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml)
when a measured limitation has a validation plan, sample threshold and rollback
path.

## Security

Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability. Follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and use
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/SignalLayerLabs/Blum/security/advisories/new).

## Financial boundary

Maintainers cannot provide personal investment advice, trade approval,
guaranteed-return support or assistance connecting the open-source project to
real-money execution. BLUM's supported operational boundary is research and
paper trading.
